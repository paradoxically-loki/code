\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Motivation}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item The core motivation is as follows: \textbf{It is deeply unethical to seriously harm sentient beings for trivial reasons such as taste, pleasure, habit, or convenience}
        \item It is also similarly problematic to pay others to harm sentient beings on our behalf.
        \item And, whenever we try to take any product/service from animals, we almost always have to mistreat (read at torture) them. 
        \item Therefore, using animal products/services for trivial reasons is unethical.
        \item And obviously, we should not do unethical things.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Veganism}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Turns out, we harm the most number of animals for food (98+\%), followed by clothing, entertainment, and research.
        \item So, if you buy the argument on the previous slide, and you don't have any honest non-trivial reasons, you are morally required to make some lifestyle changes.
        \item The new lifestyle would be such that you don't buy animal products/services for trivial reasons.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Objection 1}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{But, isn't it my personal choice what I consume or not?}
        \item It is your definitely your personal choice, but it is not your personal matter.
        \item If there were a cat sitting here peacefully, it is my personal choice to move my leg around and kick it. But is not my personal matter, as there is a victim involved, the poor cat.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Objection 2}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{What about nutrition/protein? Is Vegan diet nutritiously adequate?}
        \item Fortunately, it is. We have established research confirming that a fully plant-based diet is perfectly adequate for all ages/gender/demographics.
        \item Plant Based diet lacks Vit B12. So, you'll have to supplement that. We make B12 from from algae.
        \item Most Indians are anyway B12 deficient, so it's a good thing you supplement that, even if you're not vegan.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Objection 3}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{But Plants also feel pain!}
        \item It is unclear whether plants \textit{feel} pain. 
        \item Plants certainly respond to stimuli but that's different from pain.
        \item Also, plants lack CNS, so it is unlikely they \textbf{suffer}.
        \item But even if I grant you that plants do suffer. It would still make sense to consume plants. 
        \item This is because most crops are grown to feed animals which we then consume. 
        \item This conversion of energy from plants to animals is inefficient and we end up killing lot more plants than we otherwise would.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Moving on}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item You may come with many such arguments.
        \item But, I encourage you to think critically or search up their counters online.
        \item It is difficult to argue against the central idea of Veganism.
        \item And I understand that the conclusion of the idea is pretty demanding.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{What can I do if I can't go Vegan?}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Try as much as you can. 
        \item Each time you avoid an animal product/service, think of it as saving a life.
        \item Talk to your friends/family about the idea and try to convince them.
        \item At least, don't encourage them to try out more animal products if they already don't.
        \item You may also think about offsetting your purchases. 
        \item There are highly effective charities working to save/improve animals' lives.
        \item And money donated to such charities goes pretty far.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
