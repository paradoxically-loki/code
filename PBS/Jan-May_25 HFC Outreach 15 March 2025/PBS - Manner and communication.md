1. Be very very polite.
2. Introduce yourself and the event very briefly.  
	 > Fairy: Hi, I'm Fairy from Plant Based Society, here we're asking people some interesting question on animal ethics and give out free      goodies. It'll take around 3-4 minutes. May I know your names? 
	 > Bee: I'm Bee. 
	 > Fairy: Great, we're going to begin with our first question. Do you think it's morally wrong to punch a bottle, pillow, etc? 
 3. Be aware about the common arguments against veganism and have some research ready.
 4. Some people may deviate from animal ethics to say, casteism. Don't debate casteism. Or, some people may argue that Allah has permitted them to eat meat or Krishna drank milk. Don't start bashing Allah or Krishna or make the conversation about God.
 5. Remember, this is your conversation, you should be leading it and not the other person. 
 6. Try not to debate as much as possible, remember our point is not to prove anyone wrong or evil. Our objective is to make people aware, think and question these things.
 7. In any case, you feel it's tough to handle or the person is very annoying, or you think you don't have good counters against him, ask me to talk with them.
 8. There may be some jerks, try to politely push them to me.
 9. Remember to not sound racist, casteist, sexist, etc. This is very important as some people get annoyed very easily.
10. Don't freak out about these rules, these are only to prepare you for the worst, which doesn't happen. Most people are nice and want to have a good time discussing (this is especially true about group conversations, when a group a friends come to discuss. Try to have fun with them and acknowledge everyone's point and push them to further question their beliefs.)
11. Do thank them after the conversation, and ask them to scan the QR code to know more about the concept and PBS' resources and community.

Also, dress well. Nothing fancy but definitely not shorts, etc. Basically, be presentable. It matters a lot.
